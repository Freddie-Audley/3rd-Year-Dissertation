\section{Objectives}
\label{sec:Objectives}

\begin{flushleft} % Enforces a ragged-right edge for this section
\large

\subsection*{Research}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Review the literature on QML, Fourier representations of parameterised quantum circuits, and generalisation in quantum models.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Implementation}
\subsubsection*{Must}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Develop a reproducible pipeline to construct, train and evaluate parameterised quantum circuits on a supervised learning task
    \item Support gradient-based optimisation and record the training configuration, model parameters and evaluation results
    \item Implement a method to estimate or calculate the Fourier spectrum of the selected models
    \item Train multiple models that exhibit variation in their Fourier sparsity
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Should}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Allow the choice of optimiser and relevant hyperparameters
    \item Extend the experiments to a second learning task or circuit architecture
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Could}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Extend the pipeline to more complex datasets
    \item Investigate alternative quantum circuit architectures
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Analysis}
\subsubsection*{Must}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Evaluate the training loss, test loss and generalisation gap for each model
    \item Investigate the relationship between Fourier sparsity and the observed generalisation gap
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Should}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Repeat experiments using different initialisations to assess the variability of results
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Could}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Investigate generalisation under a distribution shift
    \item Produce rigorous proofs of generalisation bounds from Fourier sparsity
    \item Investigate whether other properties of the Fourier spectrum provide additional explanatory power for observed generalisation behaviour
\end{itemize}

\end{flushleft}
