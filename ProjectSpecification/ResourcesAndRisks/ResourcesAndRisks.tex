\section{Resources \& Risks}
\label{sec:ResourcesAndRisks}

\subsection{Resources}
\large{\raggedright
At the time of writing, I have already learned the fundamentals of QC from the Quantum Information and Computation lecture notes by Richard Jozsa\cite{Jozsa19} (sections 1 and 2), and completed the first three modules of the \href{https://pennylane.ai/codebook#modules}{\color{blue} PennyLane Codebook}. Further materials used will be referenced in later reports.\\
~\\
I am using \href{https://github.com/Freddie-Audley/3rd-Year-Dissertation}{\color{blue} GitHub} as my version control to maintain backups in the event of data loss, and to provide an insight into my workings over the course of the project through, for example, the commit history.\\
~\\
My available hardware include a PC and laptop, though neither are suited to QML training beyond implementing the pipeline. As previously mentioned, I intend to use the DCS Batch Compute system\footnotemark[1] to train the larger scale models from which data will be collected.
\par
}

\subsection{Risks}
\large{\raggedright
Small scale experiments may not be  sufficiently expressive to support interpretation. Using the DCS Batch Compute system\footnotemark[1] will mitigate issue as it will allow for larger scale QML training.\\
~\\
As this is an unfamiliar topic, there will be a delay for initial research. The timetable is structured to reflect this issue, including buffers for when it is not feasible to work on this project consistently (e.g. when there are upcoming coursework deadlines).
\par
}
